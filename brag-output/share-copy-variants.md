## Devlog / Show HN
**Title:** Show HN: Intake – describe a form, get a real Google/Microsoft Form

I got tired of building forms one dropdown at a time, so I built the interface I
wanted: you describe the form in natural language — including the follow-up
logic ("if someone needs accommodation, ask what type") — and Intake builds it
in Google Forms or Microsoft Forms, in your own account, with the branching
already wired.

It's not a form builder and not a replacement for Google/Microsoft Forms. It's
the natural-language interface for the forms you already use: Intake only ever
touches a provider after you explicitly connect it, and the finished form is a
normal Google/Microsoft Form — edit it, share it, collect responses there,
exactly like a form you built by hand.

Describe it. Build it. Use it.
